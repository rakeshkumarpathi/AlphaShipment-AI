# Shipment Operations SOP

## Delivery Delay Handling

For shipments delayed beyond the expected delivery timeline, operations should first verify the latest carrier status and identify the recorded delay reason.

For medium-severity delays, request an updated carrier status and continue monitoring.

For high-severity delays, escalate the shipment to the operations team and request a carrier update.

For critical delays, immediately escalate the shipment through the critical exception process.

## Priority Shipments

High and critical priority shipments require increased monitoring when a delivery exception occurs.

Critical priority shipments with significant delays should be escalated immediately.

## Customer Communication

Customer communication should be considered when a shipment exception has a material impact on the expected delivery or when the operational escalation policy requires customer notification.
