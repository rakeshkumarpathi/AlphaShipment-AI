# Weather and Port Disruption Policy

## Weather Disruptions

Weather conditions may contribute to shipment delays when the shipment record indicates a relevant weather condition.

Weather should be treated as contextual evidence rather than automatically determining the root cause.

## Port Congestion

Port congestion can contribute to shipment delays for shipments passing through affected ports.

Operations should consider the recorded port congestion status together with the shipment's delay reason and status.

## Operational Response

Weather and port disruptions may require increased monitoring, carrier status updates, or escalation depending on the deterministic severity assigned to the shipment.
